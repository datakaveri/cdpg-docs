Flow: Policy Lifecycle

What happens to a grant from creation to revocation — including the event pipeline that makes a policy enforced rather than merely recorded.

Validation at creation​

CheckEnforced against
The resource existsCatalogue
The granter owns it, or is org admin of its organisationCatalogue ownership + token roles
Requested access types are supported by the resourceCatalogue access-type list
No overlapping active grant existsPolicy records
Expiry is in the future (and bounded)Policy rules

After it ends​

Either way a policy ends — revocation or expiry — the full history of who had access, granted by whom, and when remains queryable. See the Audit service.
