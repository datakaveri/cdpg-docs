## Notes

- **Grants can also come from the [Marketplace](../components/marketplace.md)** — a successful purchase creates the same policy automatically (steps 2–4 collapse into the order flow).
- **Propagation is asynchronous but fast** — typically well under a second. If access is denied right after a grant, see [Troubleshooting](../guides/troubleshooting.md#policy-created-but-access-still-denied-403).
- **Expiry needs no action** — enforcement stops at the expiry timestamp automatically ([Policy Lifecycle](./policy-lifecycle.mdx)).
